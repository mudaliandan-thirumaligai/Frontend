Who is an Acharya?

"AchArya means the one who is the foremost among the Vaidhikas, who has devotion to Bhagavan, who is without egoism, who understands the thirumantram according to the correct meaning... who has no interest in artha and kAma but yet lives within the world and follows the dharma."

— Sri Periyavachan Pillai

What is Samashrayanam?

SamAshrayanam means 'to approach (AchAryA) with all sincerity'. During this process, the AchAryA initiates a person, irrespective of caste, creed or sex, as his sishyA.

It is a profound commitment from the sishya to live as per the wishes of the AchAryA, link to the Sri Vaishnava sampradayam, and perform Pancha SamskAram (Five Purifications) to become a "Sri VaishnavA".

The Five Purifications

"thApa: pundra: tathA nAma: mantrO yAgas cha panchama:"

1

Thapam

Purification of the Body

The first stage involves embossing the impressions of heated Chakra (Discus) and Sankha (Conch) on the arms, near the shoulders, signifying being the property of the Lord.

Sudarshana ChakrA

"Oh! Sudarshana MahAJwAlA, as lusturous as crores of Sun, please show me, an ignorant one, the path of Lord Vishnu"

SudaRshana Mahajwala Koti SooRya SamapraBha | Ajnaana Andhasyame Nithyam Vishno MaaRgam PradaRahaya ||

Panchajanyam

"Oh! PAnchajanyA! You are capable of driving away the sins of individuals through your sound. Please protect me, a sinner, from this samsArA"

Paanjajanya Nija Dhwaana Dwastha paathaka Sanchaya | Traayhimam aapinan Ghora SamsaaraRNava Paathinam ||

2

Urdhva Pundram

Wearing the Sacred 12 Thiruman

Representing the presence of Sriman Narayana and Sri Mahalakshmi in our body. We wear 12 Urdhva Pundrams representing the 12 sub-forms of Narayana.

Vasudeva Sankarsana Pradyumna Anirudha
Kesava Govinda Trivikrama Hrishikesha
Narayana Vishnu Vamana Padmanaba
Madhava Madhusudhana Sridhara Damodara

The White Thiruman (Clay) should follow the outline of Narayana's Lotus foot. The Red stripe (Sri Choornum) in the middle represents MangaLam and PirAtti (Mother Mahalakshmi). It should always be worn according to your Acharya's lineage instructions.

3

Namam

Assuming the Identity of a Servant

The AchAryA bestows a new name, adding 'DASAN' at the end, identifying the individual as an eternal servant to Sriman NArAyanA and Swami RAmAnujA.

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan

Eternal servant of the Lord and Swami Ramanuja

Prapatti (Total Surrender) is the path in Kali Yuga. Swami Ramanuja opened the gates of Vaikuntam for all who seek it with sincerity, regardless of their background.

4

Mantram

The Secret Truths (Rahasya Traya)

Mantra SamskAram is the most critical purification. The Acharya whispers the three sacred secrets (Rahasya Traya) into the sishya's ear.

Charama Slokam

Bhagavad Gita 18.66

Charama Slokam

"Sarvadharmaan Parith yajya Mam Ekam Charanam Vraja |
Aham tva Sarva Papebhyo Moksha ishyami Masuchaha ||"

Meaning: "Abandoning all righteous deeds, seek me as your sole refuge, I will liberate you from all sins, Do not grieve."

5

Ijyai

Service through Worship (Thiruaradhana)

This final samskAram empowers the sishya to perform Bhagavad ArAdhanam (Ritual Worship). All previous purifications culminate in making the individual eligible for these direct services.
