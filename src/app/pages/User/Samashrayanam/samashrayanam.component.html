Who is called as Acharya?

AchArya means the one who is the foremost among the Vaidhikas (vaidikAgresara), who has devotion to Bhagavan, who is without egoism, who understands the thirumantram according to the correct meaning — that thirumantram is the essence of all the Vedas, the case of all mantras, the document for the inseparable relationship of the soul and the Lord, the savior of Mumukshus, who has no interest in artha and kAma but yet lives within the world and follows the dharma, artha, kAma (lokaparigraha) — and hence practices dharma, artha and kAma.

— Sri Periyavachan Pillai

What is Samashrayanam?

SamAshrayanam means “to approach (AchAryA) with all sincerity.”

During SamAshrayanam, the AchAryA initiates a person, irrespective of caste, creed or sex, as his sishyA.

It is a commitment from the sishya that he or she will live as per the wishes of the AchAryA. Thus the person gets the link to the Sri Vaishnava sampradayam.

Pancha SamskAram (five Purifications) is performed and then he becomes a “Sri VaishnavA”.

thApa: pundra: tathA nAma: mantrO yAgas cha panchama:

The Five Samskarams

1. Thapam

To emboss the impressions of heated Chakra and Sankha on the arms (near the shoulders).

The emblem of Sudarshana ChakrA (Discus) is heated and embossed on the right hand, by chanting:

SudaRshana Mahajwala Koti SooRya SamapraBha |
Ajnaana Andhasyame Nithyam Vishno MaaRgam PradaRahaya ||

Meaning: Oh! Sudarshana MahAJwAlA, as lustrous as crores of Sun, please show me, an ignorant one, the path of Lord Vishnu.

Then, the emblem of Panchajanyam (Conch) is heated and embossed on the left hand, by chanting:

Paanjajanya Nija Dhwaana Dwastha paathaka Sanchaya |
Traayhimam aapinan Ghora SamsaaraRNava Paathinam ||

Meaning: Oh! PAnchajanyA! You are capable of driving away sins through your sound. Please protect me from this samsArA.

2. Urdhva Pundram

From the Para-Vasudeva (Sri Vaikuntam) form, Narayana emanates into the four vyuha forms and further into three under each Vyuha.

Vasudeva Sankarsana Pradyumna Anirudha
Kesava Govinda Trivikrama Hrishikesha
Narayana Vishnu Vamana Padmanaba
Madhava Madhusudhana Sridhara Damodara

Placement of 12 Thirumans

1. Forehead 4. Front neck (middle) 7. Neck (right) 10. Neck (left)
2. Lower abs (middle) 5. Lower abs (right) 8. Lower abs (left) 11. Back (lower spinal)
3. Chest 6. Right arm 9. Left arm 12. Back (upper spinal)

The Thiruman (white) should resemble the outline of Narayana's Lotus Feet. Since red color is for mangaLam (PirAtti), Sri Choornam is worn in red. SishyAs should follow their respective Acharyas.

3. Namam

The AchAryA adds the name “DASAN” at the end of the disciple’s name, to indicate that he is forever the servant of Lord Sriman Narayana and Bhagavad Ramanuja.

After Pancha Samskaram, whenever we prostrate before Acharya or Lord, we must identify ourselves as “Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan”.

Prapatti (Surrendering) is the only way in Kali Yuga to reach Sri Vaikuntam (moksham). It was Bhagavad Ramanuja who opened the gates of Sri Vaikuntam for anyone, irrespective of caste, creed or sex.

4. Mantram

Initiation of the Rahasya Traya Mantras:

  • Thirumantram — revealed by Lord as Sage Narayana to his disciple Nara in Badrikasrama.
  • Dvayam — revealed by Lord to His consort Sri in Thirupaarkadal.
  • Charama slokam — revealed by Lord Krishna to Arjuna on the chariot in the battlefield of Kurukshetra.

Eventhough all the five samskarams are highly important, Mantra Samskaram is the most important amongst them. The Rahasya traya (3 secrets) mantras are initiated to the sishya. They are the Thirumantram (Ashtaksharam), Dvayam and the Charama slokam.

Please learn Thirumantram and Dvaya Mantram from Acharya during Samashrayanam as he is one who is Qualified to teach it to Sishyas

Note:
Thirumantram can be recited only in the morning after bath.
Dvayam, there is no restrictions. Tell as many times regardless of place and time.

Thirumantram japam must be performed daily. Thirumantram japam gives all benefits that one can derive from different mantras. More importantly, it facilitates the bondage between the person performing the japam and Sriman Narayana for Saranagati (Prapatti).

Dvaya Mantram consists of two lines. It is also called as the 'Mantra Ratna' (Gem/Jewel amongst all the Mantras). This helps one to perform Saranagati unto Sriman Narayana. Once this mantra is initiated by the Acharya, it should be recited constantly within ourselves. It is in fact an instruction from Bhagavad Ramanuja that Dvayam should be chanted for maximum time possible.

Charama slokam is the famous sloka of Srimad Bhagavad Gita. It is a secret because of its invaluable hidden truth. Sriman Narayana during His Krishna Avatara did the Gita Upadesa to all the people through Arjuna. Since Bhakti Yoga was highly difficult even for Arjuna, Krishna out of His Mercy asked him (all the people) to perform 'Prapatti', as a means of God-Realisation.

Sarvadharmaan Parithyajya Mam Ekam Charanam Vraja |
Aham tva Sarva Papebhyo Mokshaishyami Masuchaha ||

Meaning: Abandon all righteous deeds and seek Me as your sole refuge. I will liberate you from all sins. Do not grieve.

5. Ijyai

Thiruaradhana Kramam booklet is available (PDF). CDs will be available at Singaperumal Koil Thirumaligai once ready.

This samskaram is very important in many angles. Thapa samskaram makes the sishya eligible to start performing various kainkaryams to Sriman Narayana. The pundra samskaram makes a person eligible to wear the dvadasa Urdhvapundram, which is a must while performing kainkaryams like Bhagavad Aradhanam. Nama samskaram makes the sishya get the intimate relationship with Sriman Narayana & Bhagavad Ramanuja, which is again important while performing kainkaryams. The mantras initiated during the mantra samskaram are used to please Sriman Narayana while performing kainkaryams like Bhagavad Aradhanam. So, all the samskarams are ultimately aimed to make the sishya perform the kainkaryams like Bhagavad Aradhanam properly.

While doing Bhagavad Aradhanam to Sriman Narayana, one is also reminded about the Lord in various Divya Desams, the nectarian anubhava of Azhwars, mercy of our Acharyas etc. All types of spiritual emotions can be nicely poured out during the Bhagavad Aradhanam, thereby bringing immense divine ecstacy. This makes one get rid of the sins that propel a person to engage in materialistic pleasures and makes him deeply attached to the Lotus Feet of the Supreme Lord Sriman Narayana.
