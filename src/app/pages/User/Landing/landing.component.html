About Thirumaligai

Sri Mudaliandan Swamy Thirumaligai is dedicated to preserving spiritual values, promoting dharma, and guiding devotees through sacred practices rooted in centuries-old tradition.

Through teachings, festivals, and community service, the Thirumaligai serves as a spiritual anchor for devotees seeking guidance, wisdom, and connection to our rich heritage.
