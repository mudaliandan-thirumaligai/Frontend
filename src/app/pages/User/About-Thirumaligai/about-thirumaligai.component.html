Sri Mudaliandan Swami Thirumaaligai

Preserving the sacred Acharya lineage and spiritual legacy of Swami Mudaliandan, the foremost disciple of Sri Ramanujacharya.

Sri Mudaliandan Swami Thirumaaligai represents a revered spiritual lineage rooted directly in the life and service of Swami Mudaliandan, also known as Dasarathy. He was the divine nephew and most trusted disciple of Sri Ramanujacharya, revered as the living embodiment of his Acharya’s will and service.

In Sri Vaishnava tradition, the term “Thirumaaligai” signifies far more than a physical residence. It denotes an Acharya parampara — a sacred custodianship of sampradayic values, practices, and teachings passed down faithfully across generations.

Swami Mudaliandan’s devotion was so complete that Sri Ramanujacharya is said to have regarded him as his own Tridandam — the sacred staff of renunciation. Entrusted with temple administration, disciple guidance, and sampradaya protection, Mudaliandan Swami played a central role in shaping the early foundations of Sri Vaishnavism.

The Mudaliandan Swami Thirumaaligai continues this legacy through unwavering commitment to Acharya Kainkaryam, Guruparampara anushtanam, and the propagation of Visishtadvaita philosophy as taught by Sri Ramanujacharya.

Today, the Thirumaaligai stands as a beacon of tradition, discipline, and devotion — guiding aspirants on the timeless path of surrender, humility, and service to Bhagavan and Acharya.
