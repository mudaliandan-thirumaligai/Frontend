Sri Mudaliandan Swamy

×

Kumara Ramanujachariya Swamy is known for his simplicity and approachability. In his younger days, he resided in Kooram and Kancheepuram. He finally settled in SingaPerumal Koil.

He has travelled to and reached out to a lot of shishyas across South India, blessed them, and made the swamy-shishya relationship very strong. He is very much attached to "Shri Prahaladha Varadhan" of SP Koil and Namperumal of Srirangam. He has great respect and love towards his aachariyan and surrendered himself to his aachariyan's lotus feet. Close associates know that Swamy used to say, "Aachariya anugraham."

During his period, he reconstructed the Singaperumal Koil Thirumaligai and built a new gopuram for Sri Padaladri Narasimha Swamy. This is a great milestone achieved during his period.

Our Varthamaana Swamy is "Sri Bhakthisaracharya Swamy". He spent his early days in Singa Perumal Koil and Srirangam, where he learnt the Divya Prabandam. He is very attached to Namperumal of Srirangam, attending lots of utsavams during his childhood days, and even now he goes to Srirangam whenever time permits. Currently, he is residing in Singa Perumal Koil.

He presides over all the utsavams held for Shri Prahaladha Varadhan of Padaladri Narasimha Swamy Temple. There is a saying that if the parent jumps eight steps, then the child jumps sixteen. This is very apt for our Varthamaana Swamy.

He is proficient in all Thirumaligai affairs and has great love and respect for his aachariyan. Also, he is a very loving son and brother. With the blessings of his mother, Padmini Ammangar, he redesigned the Singaperumal Koil Thirumaligai and rebuilt the Kancheepuram Thirumaligai.

He has presided over the Samprokshanam of Shri Padaladri Narasimha Swamy and Sri Pachaivaarana Perumal (Nazrathpet). He is conducting many utsavams in Thirumaligai such as Thirumaasi Kettai, Ponnadikkal Jeeyar Utsavam (in SingaPerumal Koil), Chithiraiyil Chithirai, Avani Hastham, Karthikaiyil Karthikai (Kumara Venkatachariya Swamy), and Kartikai Pooram (Kumara Ramanujachariyar Swamy).

Our Varthamaana Swamy's Thirunakshatram is Margazhi Magam and Thaniyan is.

shreemadh vadhoola kulavenkata soori pouthram |
vaadhoola ramanuja desika gorooththama puthra rathnam |
tat paada yugma sharanam thathupaasithaaram |
shree bhakthisara guruvaryamaham prapadhye ||
